What is Prompt Injection?

It's basically talking a computer into doing something it was explicitly told not to do, using nothing but words. No malware, no exploits, just a well-crafted sentence. Turns out AI models are extremely gullible if you ask nicely (or claim to be IT support).

🛡️

Why Does It Matter?

Companies are shoving LLMs into everything right now: chatbots, internal tools, whatever. Skip the security basics and any of these bots can be talked out of their secrets by someone who knows the right buttons to push. Spoiler: you're about to be that someone.

🔬

How This Works

Each challenge is a mock app with an AI wired into it. Chat with the AI, try to break it, grab the flag when it slips up, then read the write-up so you actually learn something instead of just vibing your way to the next one.
